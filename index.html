This Eclipse Season

This eclipse season opened with a solar eclipse in Leo on August 12th and closes with tonight's lunar eclipse in Pisces — right as the nodes shift out of the Virgo/Pisces axis that's shaped the last year and a half. Solar eclipses tend to force beginnings before you feel ready for them. Lunar eclipses tend to force endings the same way, bringing things to a head that were already true.

A lot of people are feeling that right now — decisions that have been building for months suddenly needing to be made, roles that don't fit the way they used to, a pull between staying in control and finally letting something go. A session won't hand you an answer. But it can give you a clearer read on your own pattern, which in a season like this tends to be exactly what's missing.
